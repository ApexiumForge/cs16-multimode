TORBESHI | HOW TO PLAY

Choose your mode in /menu.

CLASSIC

Team rounds with bomb or hostage objectives.
Friendly fire is off.

DEATHMATCH

Every player is an enemy. Respawn and keep fighting.
/dm Choose & save weapons   /top Scores

ARMS RACE

2 gun kills or 1 knife kill advances a level.
Getting knifed drops you a level. Final knife kill wins.
/ar Your progress   /artop Leaders

KNIFE 35HP

Team rounds. Knife only, 35 HP, no armor or buying.
Knife maps load automatically. Respawn next round.

AWP ONLY

Free-for-all sniper combat with AWP and knife.

HE BATTLE

Unlimited HE grenades. No guns or buying.
Allies are protected. First to 30 enemy kills wins.
/top Standings

DM / Arms Race / HE: 2-second spawn protection.
Attacking ends protection. Spawns favor space from players.
/crosshair Customize & save your crosshair.
Players stay solid and fully visible.
