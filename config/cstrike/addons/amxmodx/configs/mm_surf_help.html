COMBAT SURF
Ride the ramps

Jump onto a sloped ramp. Hold A on its right side, or D on its left side. Look smoothly along the ramp. Release W while surfing; use gentle mouse turns to build speed.

Fight and respawn

Fight the opposing team and pick up map weapons. Teammates stay solid and cannot hurt each other. Falling causes no damage. Respawn protection lasts 2 seconds and ends when you attack.

Your controls

/surf - auto-bhop, speed display and next-spawn weapon
/respawn or /restart - return to spawn
Hold jump to auto-bhop when enabled. No checkpoints or teleport shortcuts during combat.
