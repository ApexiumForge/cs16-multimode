TORBESHI | MODE GUIDE
CLASSIC
Full teams can /ready immediately. /score shows the match.

DEATHMATCH
FFA, first to 50. /dm chooses weapons; /reuse equips your saved loadout. Choices survive reconnects.

ARMS RACE
2 gun kills per level; 1 knife kill advances and demotes the victim. Final knife wins. /ar /artop. Vote to replay or change maps after a win.

SNIPER ARENA
Vote AWP or Scout at match start. FFA, first to 20. /awp shows rules; admins can toggle scoped-only shots.

HE BATTLE
Unlimited grenades. /he shows Team or FFA rules. Target 30 team kills in Teams, individual kills in FFA. Allies protected in Teams.

KNIFE 35HP
Team elimination, first to 5 rounds. /knifescore /ktop

FOOTBALL
E/left click shoots; R/right click passes. First to 5 goals or 10 minutes. /football adjusts power; /ftop shows goals/assists. Throw-ins, corners and goal kicks reserve pickup for 2 seconds.

COMBAT SURF
Ride ramps, collect weapons and fight. Quick respawns, no fall damage. /surf options; /surfhelp guide.

/skins /knife /crosshair
DM, Arms Race and HE: attacking ends spawn protection.
