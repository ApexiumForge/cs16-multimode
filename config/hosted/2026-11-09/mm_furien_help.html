Furien

Terrorists: Fast knives, low gravity and silent footsteps. Stand still with your knife to become invisible. Attacking briefly reveals you. Plant the bomb or eliminate CTs.

Counter-Terrorists: Use guns to hunt Furiens and defend the bomb sites. Type /furien to choose M4A1, AK47, MP5 or M3, each with a USP.

Normal rounds and base spawns. A CT win swaps teams next round. Both sides start with 100 HP; CTs get full armor.
