Zombie Plague
Classic humans vs zombies.

HUMANS

Choose normal guns, hold a position together and survive the infection. Shots push zombies back. HE grenades burn, flashbangs freeze and smoke grenades light the area.

ZOMBIES

Infect humans with your claws. Choose Classic, Raptor, Big, Leech or Tight Zombie; your choice applies on your next infection. Armor protects humans until it runs out.

AMMO PACKS

Earn packs through damage and infections. Spend them on antidote, temporary zombie madness, night vision, armor or Golden AK47 (+20% damage). No jetpacks or futuristic weapons.

CONTROLS

M or /zpmenu - zombie menu
/zclass - zombie classes
/rules - this guide
N - night vision when available
F - flashlight

Mostly infection rounds, with occasional Nemesis or Survivor rounds when at least four players are present. Entering or leaving Zombie mode reloads the map.
