COMBAT SURF
Ride the ramps

Jump onto a sloped ramp. Hold A on its right side, or D on its left side. Look smoothly along the ramp. Release W while surfing; use gentle mouse turns to build speed.

Fight and respawn

Start with a USP (CT) or Glock (T), a knife and no armor. Money stays at $16,000. Buy weapons or collect them around the map and fight the opposing team. Teammates stay solid and cannot hurt each other. Falling causes no damage. Respawn protection lasts 2 seconds and ends when you attack.

Your controls

/surf - auto-bhop and speed display
/respawn or /restart - return to spawn
Hold jump to auto-bhop when enabled. No checkpoints or teleport shortcuts during combat.
